## Retired approach

The first implementation flood-filled two source-truncated handset fragments to transparency. Although it preserved source truth, the open phone edges read as broken in the rendered hero. Those derived PNGs are no longer shipped or referenced.

## Constraints

- Do not reintroduce transparent handset fragments from source artwork that already truncates the device.
- Do not fabricate missing handset pixels or synthetic product screens.
- A future literal complete-device composition requires official full-device source captures. Until then, keep the supplied store artwork inside honest, complete evidence plates.
